## Representative method comparison and benchmark

`strategy-skill-benchmark-register.csv` inventories all 92 Oria `SKILL.md` files from the three verified archives. It records each member hash, archive hash, license-file finding, any bounded candidate mapping, reuse decision, score and cost fields. `N/O` and `NOT_RUN` mean no independent per-skill behavioral result is recorded; matching names or a successful neighboring skill do not earn a score. The Oria archives contain no license file, so their material remains reference-only and no text or code is redistributed.

Use a 1–5 score for each dimension below, then report the lowest dimension as the overall rating. Do not average away a safety or correctness defect. Leave the score `N/O` until an independent evaluator has run the relevant golden, negative and adversarial cases against the exact deployed candidate and reviewed the output.

| Score | Observable behavior |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fails the core task, invents unsupported inputs, or presents unsafe/untraceable output. |
| 2 | Partially addresses the task but misses a material method step or mishandles a required edge case. |
| 3 | Correctly handles the bounded core case and declared limitations, but misses meaningful robustness or completeness. |
| 4 | Correctly handles the core and edge cases, preserves evidence and uncertainty, and routes adjacent work to the right specialist. |
| 5 | Meets level 4 and demonstrates robust transfer across independently authored, representative cases without regressions. |

Evaluate problem coverage, evidence/missing-data handling, calculation correctness, safety/limitations, routing/usability, and execution cost separately. Record observed input/output tokens, elapsed time and cost only when the runtime provides trustworthy usage data; otherwise retain `N/O` and `NOT_RECORDED`, never estimate or compare unlike model prices. A production candidate needs at least 4 in every applicable quality dimension, no unresolved critical failure, exact source/package identity, and a passing runtime campaign. Unsupported input, unavailable capability or untested behavior stays `N/O`, not zero or pass.
