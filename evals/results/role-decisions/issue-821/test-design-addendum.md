## Oracle and evidence gaps

Existing `tests/product-intelligence.test.mjs` contains named checks for malformed rows and type-specific coverage/date bounds, but it must be reviewed against the specific cases above; test-file presence alone does not establish the required oracle, route behavior or PASS. Additional tests are needed for per-resource and deployment-scoped cache/LKG, actual route and Worker shared identity resolution, client refresh/auth loss, source-independent failure, exact 90/91-day boundaries, malformed rows in both resources and reopen-history coverage. Use independently listed source IDs, UTC intervals, coverage states and timestamps; do not derive expected results by calling the function under test. Preserve prior fixture expectations unless the new documented semantic distinction is independently justified.

A unit mock or CI result cannot establish any of these. The separate Test Executor and Independent Reviewer must assess the final exact candidate and record commands, fixture digests, output and exit statuses; release evidence must reconcile the actual deployed identity.
